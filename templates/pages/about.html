About GrandhaShare

Grandha means "book". Share is the idea of passing books on.

Our story

Textbooks are expensive, and many are used for only one semester. GrandhaShare started as a student project to help Kerala college students pass books from seniors to juniors, organised by university, course and semester.

What we do

Students can buy, swap or donate used textbooks. Sellers accept or reject requests, and contact details are shared only after acceptance.

Our commitment

  • Keep the platform free and simple for students.
  • Protect your contact details until you accept a request.
  • Remove spam and fake listings reported by users.

GrandhaShare does not handle payments or delivery. Students meet on campus and settle details themselves.
